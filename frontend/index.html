                    Understanding Chess Beyond Raw Centipawns

                    Standard engines evaluate positions assuming perfect 3500-rated play. ChessMax introduces three breakthrough features to reveal human difficulty, realistic move findability, and true decision-making skills.

                    FEATURE 01

                    1. Why Volatility Bar

                    Measuring positional difficulty, minefields, and practical danger beyond engine evaluation.

                    Stockfish's eval ranks positions by best play. It can't see that one of two equally "equal" positions is a bone-dry handshake while the other is a single-move minefield. The volatility bar measures that difference: how punishing it is to choose the wrong move.

                    What V is not

                    • V is not difficulty. A knife-edge position is V-high, but a strong player who has seen the pattern finds the move in seconds. V measures the cost of guessing wrong, not the cognitive load of guessing right.
                    • V is not a blunder predictor. It tells you what a blunder would cost, not how likely either side is to make one.
                    • V is not a style score. "Sharp" and "quiet" describe openings; V answers — how punishing is the wrong move right now?

                    Green bar: the position plays itself. Red bar: you're walking on eggshells.

                    FEATURE 02

                    2. Why Findability Score

                    How humanly possible is the engine's top move to find?

                    Traditional engine reviews scold you for missing a +3.5 top move that relies on a 7-ply quiet computer sacrifice. But no human below master strength would ever spot that line! Reviewing human play against 3500-rated Stockfish depth produces frustration rather than actionable feedback.

                    Findability Score measures human possibility. Driven by rating-conditioned human policy models (Maia-3 / Maia-2) and search-visibility factors (depth d*, forcing checks/captures, and line narrowness), it computes the monotone human probability curve C_A(r) across ratings from 600 Elo to 2600 Elo.

                    Rating Threshold (R_find) & 5 Bands

                    R_find is the rating level where 50% of players find an acceptable move. Findability scales R_find into a 0–100 score:

                    • 90–100 Obvious Trivial captures or basic checks (R_find < 800).
                    • 70–89 Natural Sound positional moves or standard tactics (R_find ~ 1100).
                    • 45–69 Needs Thought Requires focused calculation (R_find ~ 1500).
                    • 20–44 Hard Deep calculation or quiet maneuvering (R_find ~ 1900).
                    • 0–19 Engine-Only Inhuman computer geometry (R_find > 2400).

                    Best Findable Move Recommendation

                    When the top engine move is labeled Engine-Only, ChessMax doesn't penalize you. Instead, it surfaces the strongest humanly findable alternative move—the move a human could realistically have found and played.

                    FEATURE 03

                    3. Why Puzzles 2.0

                    Eliminating the "Tactics Blindness" flaw of traditional puzzle trainers.

                    Standard puzzle trainers (Lichess, Chess.com) have a fatal flaw: you know a tactic exists! When you open a puzzle, you stop deciding whether to calculate and immediately hunt for checks, captures, and queen sacrifices. In real over-the-board games, nobody taps you on the shoulder to tell you "there's a tactic on move 22". You must decide whether a tactic is present or if you should play a solid positional move.

                    Puzzles 2.0 fixes this fundamental flaw.

                    Traditional Puzzle Trainers

                    100% Tactical Bias

                    • ❌ Every single position contains a winning tactic.
                    • ❌ Trains "tactic hunting" instead of position assessment.
                    • ❌ Gives away the answer before you even look at the board.
                    • ❌ Forces 1 single computer move even when 6 moves hold equal eval.
                    Puzzles 2.0 (ChessMax)

                    50/50 Hidden Mix + Quiet Positions

                    • ✅ Seamlessly mixes tactical puzzles with quiet positions mined from master games.
                    • ✅ Strict "No Tell" Invariant: Positions look 100% identical before your move.
                    • ✅ Eval-Loss Grading: Any move within 100cp eval loss passes quiet positions.
                    • ✅ Maia Play-Out: Play out any position against human-like neural net engines.
